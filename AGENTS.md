# Topical Knowledge Cell Report Service

This settlement sells one source-grounded report over one explicitly selected,
fresh, non-authoritative topical knowledge-cell manifest. It hires the
NotebookLM inference team for generation and hires RMN, RWIL/RDF, A2A,
ActivityPub, and x402 providers at their own borders.

Internal service is carried by A2A. External purchase uses an exact x402 quote
and direct ActivityPub delivery to the buyer; it never publishes publicly.
Payment, generation, and delivery are independently idempotent. A report is an
AI-produced, source-grounded artifact—not a truth proof or a completeness claim.
No customer receives notebook creation, corpus mutation, sharing, deletion,
publication, settlement administration, or delegation authority.
